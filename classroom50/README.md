# Classroom 50 Invites — Academic Year 2026-2027

This course is taught to three groups. Each group has its **own classroom** in Classroom 50, with its own deadlines and its own links.

> **Important:** only use the page of your own group. A link from another group's page does not work for you: Classroom 50 refuses the assignment because you are not on that classroom's roster.

| Group | Programme | Who | Invite page |
|-------|-----------|-----|-------------|
| **TI** | Toegepaste Informatica | Bachelor students Toegepaste Informatica | [Open the TI page](./TI.md) |
| **ACS** | Applied Computer Science | International Erasmus students | [Open the ACS page](./ACS.md) |
| **AO** | Afstandsonderwijs | Distance learning students | [Open the AO page](./AO.md) |

## Before your first lab

1. Accept the GitHub invitation to the **VivesMDima** organization (you receive it by email from your teacher).
2. Open your group's page and click the accept link of the lab.
3. Classroom 50 creates your private repository `<classroom>-<lab>-<your-username>` in VivesMDima.
4. Clone it, run `npm install` and `npm test`, and submit with `npm run submit`.

See [Working with Classroom 50](../01-Intro/01-course-introduction.md#working-with-classroom-50) for the full walkthrough.

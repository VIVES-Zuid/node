# Labs

Every chapter ends with a lab. Labs are autograded by Classroom 50 and give you feedback on each requirement. They do not count towards your grade; the final project and the oral defense do.

Accept a lab through your group's page in [Classroom 50 invites](./classroom50/README.md).
